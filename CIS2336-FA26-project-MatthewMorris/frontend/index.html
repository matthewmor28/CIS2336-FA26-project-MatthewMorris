About the Student Center

Welcome to the proposed University of Houston Sugar Land Student Center!

Whether you need a quiet study space or a meeting room for a group project, the student center is designed to support your academic and social experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the UH Sugar Land Student Center.

1. What are the operating hours?

The Student Center is open Monday through Thursday from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Friday from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The center is closed on Sundays and all University holidays. Hours may vary during finals week and special events.

2. Who can reserve a room?

You can reserve a room through this website using our Room Booking page. Select your preferred room, date, and time. A valid UH student or faculty ID is required. Reservations can be made up to two weeks in advance, and students may book up to 3 hours per day per room.

3. How far ahead can I book?

The demonstration booking form allows users to select a date up to 30 days in advance.

4. How long can I reserve a room?

The maximum reservation duration is three hours with exception to volume of bookings.

5. What happens if a room is booked?

If the room is already booked, you will receive a message indicating that the room is unavailable.
